# Research lineage (source claims vs design hypotheses)

- Lin et al., [Sleep-time Compute](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13171v1): anticipatory context computation and amortized query cost. Does not establish persistent weight consolidation.
- Lee et al., [Offline Recurrence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26099v3): repeated offline context passes update SSM fast weights before KV eviction. Does not imply those states equal low-rank MLP adapters.
- Behrouz et al., [Language Models Need Sleep](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03979v2): Knowledge Seeding, generalized distillation and learned synthetic rehearsal via Dreaming. Does not establish surgical MoE compilation or zero forgetting.
- Geiping et al., [Scaling up Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05171): recurrent latent depth, distinct from sleep consolidation.
- [EX0005A/Aa](../2026-11-15-ex0005/README.md): observed repair does not imply mechanistic identification; original instrument FAIL and general capability INCONCLUSIVE; subsequent separately frozen exact repair and identifiability counterexample. Preserve historical outcomes. [EX0005B](../2026-11-19-ex0005b-lm01/README.md) lineage: bounded interventions, independent evaluation and rollback; no general WSP capability claim.
- [S03 Field / Program Synthesis Doctrine](https://github.com/xxx3r/Grav_grav/blob/ce451c18bc8e9bf8527e21a2a3faa5416dd33044/relativity_engine/docs/s03/S03_FIELD_PROGRAM_SYNTHESIS_DOCTRINE.md) and [STYLE-H0](https://github.com/xxx3r/Grav_grav/blob/ce451c18bc8e9bf8527e21a2a3faa5416dd33044/relativity_engine/docs/s03/STYLE_HISTORICAL_PROGRAM_GEOMETRY.md): original proposal-only surfer map, micro/program/skill/slow-core scales, history-conditioned preferences, typed proposals and bounded durable writes. Inspiration only; SOMNIA is independent and does not inherit authority or evidence of effectiveness.
- [JAX scan](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/_autosummary/jax.lax.scan.html) / [while_loop](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/_autosummary/jax.lax.while_loop.html): static scan supports reverse-mode autodiff; dynamic while_loop does not.

Design synthesis: recurrent-depth crystallization, localized compilation, frozen token mixing + plastic expert, fast-memory-to-adapter bridge and STYLE-like selection are distinct testable hypotheses. No novelty or efficacy claim for integration. No claim of zero forgetting outside exact dependency isolation. No autonomous self-modification claim for externally implemented compiler. Paper abstract-level source verification on setup date; full independent reproductions not performed.
